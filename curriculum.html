How we learn

A curriculum built around the child.

Our program draws on Montessori-inspired principles and play-based learning — covering six developmental domains and growing with each child at their own pace.

Montessori CASA Program

Child-centered learning, grounded in observation.

The Montessori CASA program is a child-centered educational approach built on close observation of how children actually learn. It encourages creativity and curiosity, and leads children to ask questions, explore, investigate, and think for themselves.

Our program is Montessori-inspired. While we follow the CASA curriculum and materials, our educators are not AMI/AMS certified Montessori teachers.

Practical Life

Hands-on activities that build concentration and coordination through everyday skills — pouring, sweeping, dressing, and caring for the environment around them.

Sensorial

Programs that refine the five senses through specially designed materials focusing on smell, hearing, visual and taste perception, tactile awareness, and spatial reasoning.

Language & Literacy

Language development in sensitive periods, building phonological awareness, book-handling, and letter recognition through multi-sensory approaches.

Mathematics & Cultural Studies

Math concepts introduced through concrete materials, alongside cultural exploration including music, geography, science, zoology, and botany.

Six developmental domains

Growing the whole child.

Our educators actively engage with children, observing their interests and planning activities that deepen exploration. Learning is multi-sensory, hands-on, and woven through every part of the day.

01

Social Skills

Multi-cultural exposure through events, dramatic play, and literature featuring different cultures and traditions — building empathy and a sense of belonging.

02

Language & Literacy

Phonological awareness, book-handling, letter recognition, and multi-sensory learning approaches — visual, auditory, kinesthetic, and tactile.

03

Emotional Development

Clear classroom expectations, social skill reinforcement — sharing, negotiation, empathy — and age-appropriate strategies for managing frustration.

04

Creative Arts

Exploration with various textures, materials, and colour — process-led artwork that lets children express themselves without a predetermined outcome.

05

Science & Discovery

Animal classification, life cycles, plant growth, and sensory exploration — nurturing natural curiosity about the world and how it works.

06

Mathematical Thinking

Number awareness, sorting, pattern creation, and visual representation — foundations laid through concrete, tactile materials before any abstract introduction.

Our philosophy

Teachers who watch, listen, and follow the child's lead.

Our educators interact with children, observe what they are drawn to, and plan activities that expand on those interests. Learning happens through circle time, Jolly Phonics, interactive reading, and environmental exploration — always using all five senses.

We believe a love of learning is the most important thing a child can leave us with. That starts with giving children the time, space, and encouragement to ask questions, seek answers, and build confidence in themselves.
