Parent app

Your child's day, in your pocket.

The Parent app connects you with our educators in real time — daily check-ins, photos, messages, and more. Free to download.

Get it on Google Play Download on App Store

Search "Parent: Child Care App" in your app store if the links above don't take you directly.

What's in the app

Everything you need to stay close to your child's day.

Designed for busy parents and attentive educators — a simple, shared space where the day unfolds.

Direct Messaging

Send and receive messages with your child's educators throughout the day — no phone tag needed.

Daily Photos & Updates

Receive photos and activity updates from your child's day as they happen.

Daily Reports

Nap times, meals, activities, and mood — a clear record of how your child's day went.

Attendance Tracking

Sign in and out digitally, and see a history of your child's attendance at a glance.

Announcements

Centre-wide updates, event reminders, and notices — all in one place, never lost in email.

Real-Time Notifications

Instant notifications for messages and updates — so you're never out of the loop.

Getting started

You'll be set up in minutes.

We'll send you an invite when your child enrols. Here's how it works.

01

Download the app

Search "Parent: Child Care App" on Google Play or the App Store and install it — it's free.

02

Accept your invitation

We'll send an invite to the email address you registered with. Tap it to create your account and connect to your child's profile.

03

Stay connected

From day one, you'll receive updates, photos, and messages from the team. Your child's day is never more than a tap away.
