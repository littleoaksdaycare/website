Our story

A centre you can trust.

We are here because children and family matter — not as a slogan, but as the reason for everything.

The family behind Little Oaks

Hi — there are four of us, and this place matters to all of us.

Little Oaks is owned and managed by my husband and me. I manage the overall direction of the centre — making sure things run well and that our team has what they need to do their best work. The care itself is led by people who have been here for years. Our site director has been with Little Oaks for over a decade, and that kind of continuity means everything to us.

Children and family are at the heart of everything I do. This centre stood for exactly that — a place where children are genuinely known, where the pace is calm, and where the people don't rotate every few months. Being part of it wasn't a business decision. It was a calling.

We have two children of our own, and we think about the kind of world we want them to grow up in. We want Little Oaks to be here for the long haul — a place worth trusting, worth returning to, and someday, in the far-off future, worth passing down to the next generation of our own family.

— with care, the Little Oaks family

What we believe in

The people here are good.

We inherited a team of caregivers who genuinely care about their work. We didn't come to replace anything — we came to protect and support what they've built.

Stability is a gift.

Children need to trust the grown-ups around them. That starts with low staff turnover, consistent faces, and an environment where caregivers feel valued enough to stay.

Small on purpose.

We are not trying to grow into a chain. We want Little Oaks to stay exactly what it is: a small, quiet, family-run centre where your child is known by name.

Come and see for yourself.

The best way to know if we're a fit is to walk through the door. We'd love to meet you.
